VANISHING GLOBAL COLOR ENERGY AND THE POINTWISE COLLISION GAP
2026-10-15. Mathematical deduction, not Lean formalized.
Historical novelty of this formulation is unverified.

This strengthens the small-difference-set conclusion from a bound on the
number of relative permutations to a bound on their collision energy.
It uses the independently audited local square and basepoint lifting
lemmas in partial-small-difference-theorem.txt.

THEOREM. Fix eta>0. Let a properly colored bipartite simple graph have
every C4 rainbow. Embed each side injectively into the same n-point set
Omega. Take d colors, each containing at least eta*n original edges,
and choose pairwise distinct permutations extending their matchings.
Let S be this set of d permutations. Uniformly over all such graphs,
sets Omega, and choices of extensions, as d tends to infinity,

 E(S) := #{(a,b,c,e) in S^4 : a b^{-1}=c e^{-1}} = o(d^3).

There is no assumption that the completed permutation graphs are
B-colored. The original partial matchings supply all graph constraints.
There is also no solvability assumption or bound on the generated group.

PROOF.
Suppose for contradiction that E(S)>=kappa*d^3 along a sequence with
d tending to infinity, for fixed kappa>0. Apply Tao's noncommutative
Balog--Szemeredi--Gowers theorem, in its approximate-group formulation:
Theorem 5.4, implication (i) => (iv), to (S,S^{-1}).
There is a K-approximate group A and g in the permutation group with

       |A| <= B*d,        |S intersect gA| >= alpha*d,

where K,B,alpha>0 depend only on kappa. In particular |A|>=alpha*d.
This is a direct application of the stated theorem; it does not require
the two subsets supplied by a two-set BSG theorem to be identical.

Retain the colors in S intersect gA, and change the right-side labels
by g^{-1}. The representing permutations now form U subset A, with
|U|>=alpha*d. Every retained color still has at least eta*n edges,
so the retained graph has m'>=eta*n*|U| edges.

For z in Omega, use the basepoint lift from the companion note:

 F_z = {(u^{-1},v u^{-1}) : u,v in U and the original color-v
                              edge exists at row u^{-1}z}.

It is a B-colored partial group table inside A x A^2. The exact identity

                  sum_z |F_z| = |U| m'

shows that some z satisfies

 |F_z| >= |U|m'/n >= eta*|U|^2
       >= (eta*alpha^2/B^2)*|A|^2.

Since |A| tends to infinity, the local square lemma contradicts this
fixed positive density. Thus every fixed kappa is eventually impossible.
This proves the theorem.

SOURCE FOR THE ADDITIONAL INPUT
T. Tao, Product set estimates for non-commutative groups,
Theorems 5.2 and 5.4 (especially 5.4(i) => (iv)), PDF pp.15--18:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0601431

The local square lemma uses the full Breuillard--Green--Tao structure
theorem, large abelian subgroups of finite groups, and density
Hales--Jewett. No informal 2025 remark is needed for this qualitative
energy conclusion. The detailed dependencies are in the companion note.

COROLLARY 1: SUPERCONSTANT PRODUCT GROWTH.
If r(h)=#{(a,b) in S^2:a b^{-1}=h}, then

 sum_h r(h)=d^2,       sum_h r(h)^2=E(S).

By Cauchy--Schwarz,

                    |SS^{-1}| >= d^4/E(S).

Consequently |SS^{-1}|/d tends to infinity. The energy assertion is
stronger: a large support by itself would allow a positive fraction of
the products to remain concentrated in a small subset.

COROLLARY 2: ROBUSTNESS FOR DENSE CANDIDATE COUNTEREXAMPLES.
Suppose m>=epsilon*N^2, q<=D*N and |Omega|<=C*N, with fixed positive
epsilon,C,D. Discard colors with fewer than epsilon*N/(2D) edges.
At least half the edges remain. Colors represented by the same
permutation occupy disjoint subsets of its graph, so at most
2*C*D/epsilon of these retained colors have the same permutation.
Select one largest color from each duplicate class, retaining a constant
fraction of the edges. There remain d=Theta(N) distinct permutations;
each retained color has at least

                  (epsilon/(2*C*D))*|Omega|

edges. The theorem therefore forces E(S)=o(N^3) and
|SS^{-1}|/N tending to infinity, for EVERY initial completion.

COROLLARY 3: THE PRECISE LOCAL/GLOBAL OBSTRUCTION.
For x in Omega define the pointwise collision count

 E_x(S) = #{(a,b,c,e) in S^4 :
                    (a b^{-1})(x)=(c e^{-1})(x)}.

There are d^2 ordered products and at most n possible images of x.
Therefore Cauchy--Schwarz gives, for EVERY x,

                         E_x(S) >= d^4/n.

In a hypothetical dense counterexample, d=Theta(n), so the pointwise
energy is Omega(d^3) at every x while global energy is o(d^3).
Equivalently,

 sum_{a,b,c,e in S} |Fix(e c^{-1} a b^{-1})|
                      = sum_x E_x(S) >= d^4.

The quadruples with identical global products contribute only
n*E(S)=o(n*d^3). When d=Theta(n), they account for a vanishing fraction
of this lower bound. Thus the overwhelming required collision mass
must come from NONIDENTICAL products agreeing at individual points.

This does not contradict the B condition. The B condition excludes
particular repeated-color four-step words, but permits rainbow
four-cycles with four different colors. No argument here turns the
remaining pointwise agreements into global product equalities.

WHAT HAS AND HAS NOT ADVANCED
This gives an asymptotic constraint involving arbitrarily many colors,
not a new constant in an edge-density estimate. It rules out bounded
product complexity and any positive normalized global energy among
many large color classes. It does not prove the missing universal
edge bound o(N^2), because large pointwise collision counts need not
imply large global permutation energy.

INDEPENDENT AUDIT OF THE GROUP-REPRESENTATION VANISHING-DENSITY PROOF
2026-10-15. Reviewed by the formal_audit agent independently of its author.

Reviewed artifact:
group-representation-vanishing-density.txt

Verdict: its mathematical reductions pass the adversarial checks below,
conditional on the two stated established inputs (density Hales-Jewett and
the existence of abelian subgroups of unbounded size in finite groups of
unbounded order). This audit is not a Lean certificate or novelty claim.

1. The weighted cube construction does not require injectivity. Averaging
   over its two translations gives the correct density for every word
   separately, which is enough to apply DHJ. Choosing w_i outside the
   negatives of the 2^(i-1) prior subset sums prevents every nonempty
   zero-sum subset. The condition |H|>2^(r-1) suffices through step r.
   In characteristic 2, the resulting t is still nonzero and the four
   square corners are distinct. There is no hidden odd-order assumption.

2. A dense coset rectangle gH x kH yields precisely the required equal
   diagonal group-table colors. The cancellation
   (k h_2 t)(g h_1 t)^(-1)=k h_2 h_1^(-1)g^(-1)
   is valid in a nonabelian ambient group. Abelianity is used only inside
   H to obtain the square, not in that cancellation.

3. In the stabilizer lift, every original edge has exactly h lifts and
   the lifted color is exactly its injectively labeled group element.
   The potentially dangerous projected degeneracy is handled correctly:
   equal diagonal lifted colors and equal projected rows force equal
   projected columns. Then all four projected edges are the same simple
   original edge, so every lifted cross-edge must have its one color.
   That forces the lifted rows to coincide, contradicting a genuine C4.
   If the projected rows differ, the projected columns also differ and
   the original B condition directly forbids the cycle.

4. Nontransitive actions cause no density loss of an uncontrolled kind.
   From m>=epsilon N^2 and sum n_i=N, some orbit has
   m_i/n_i>=epsilon N. The bound m_i<=n_i^2/4 gives
   n_i>=4epsilon N and m_i>=4epsilon^2 N^2. Its orbit has size at least
   n_i/2, so |G| tends to infinity. With |G|<=KN, the lifted density is
   at least 4epsilon^2/K^2. This is a fixed positive density, to which
   the uniform group-table theorem applies.

5. The statement permits the left and right label sets to overlap and
   the action to be unfaithful. Neither issue affects the preceding proof.
   Separate injectivity on each side and injective color labels are used
   and must remain in the theorem. Arbitrary matching completions do not
   provide the crucial |G|=O(N) hypothesis.

Conclusion: the stated o(N^2) result is valid for the described restricted
class. Its novelty and relationship to finite-group recurrence literature
are separate questions. The result does not establish o(N^2) for arbitrary
B-colorings or solve Erdős #810.

ADDENDUM: COMMUTING-COLOR-COVER COROLLARY

Also audited commuting-color-cover-corollary.txt. Its extension to bounded
abelian-coset palette covers passes. The potentially missing injectivity
of colors after quotienting an abelian orbit is repaired correctly by
discarding classes smaller than eta N, with eta=beta/(2D). At most DN
colors then cost beta N^2/2 edges. Each quotient translation can carry
at most C/eta remaining labels, since the separate side injections give
a matching of size at most v<=CN. Selecting one largest class for each
translation retains at least beta^2 N^2/(4CD) edges, of relative density
at least beta^2/(4C^3D) in the quotient group table. The subsequent
application of the group-table theorem is therefore legitimate. The
bounded-cover assumption is essential and is not established for arbitrary
permutation palettes.

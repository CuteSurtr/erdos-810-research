Retaining the side-size profile in the endpoint recursion
2026-10-15. Proof draft, not Lean verified. External novelty not established.

RESULT. The recursive-density-bound.txt argument strengthens to

             M(N) <= (rho_profile+o(1)) N^2,
             rho_profile = 0.2984440375099955469... .

Let C_* be the unique root greater than 1 of

             9C^4+32C^3+13C^2-45C-36=0.

Then C_*=1.1854165591228241566..., alpha=1/(1+C_*)
=0.4575786688471771051..., and rho_profile is the unique root in [0,1/2]
of 4(1+alpha)rho^3-2(1+alpha)rho^2-3rho+1=0.

This is a refinement of the same proof method, not a proof of o(N^2).
The proof uses the properification lemma only to pass from proper B to
arbitrary rainbow-C4 colorings.

1. A general profile and the endpoint induction.
Normalize colors to N and let a,b be the bipartite part sizes divided by N,
with s=a+b<=1. For a nonnegative profile A(s), use the bound

                  F_A(a,b)=ab s/[s+ab(1+s A(s))].

As in recursive-density-bound.txt, a bound F_A means a uniform error o(N^2)
over all graphs and part sizes. That file proves the base profile A=0 using
the exact elementary bipartite inequality.

For C>=1 put A_C(s)=1/(1+C s). We will prove that a valid A_C profile implies
a valid A_{K(C)} profile, where

 K(C)=(19C^3+75C^2+93C+36)/(9C^3+51C^2+88C+48).             (1)

The first application to A=0 yields the valid profile A_{19/9}.

2. Concavity needed for Jensen.
Fix u<=1 and write z=k(u-k), d=1/u+A_C(u). Then

 F_A(k,u-k)=z/(1+dz),
 (d^2/dk^2)F_A(k,u-k)
      =-2/(1+dz)^2-2d(u-2k)^2/(1+dz)^3.

Because 0<=A_C<=1 and 0<=u<=1,
dz<=u/4+u^2/4<=u/2. Thus this second derivative is at most
-2/(1+u/2)^2. For the endpoint integrand

             h(k)=k^2+F_A(k,b-k)+F_A(a-k,k),

convexity of u -> (1+u/2)^(-2) gives

 h''<=2-2/(1+a/2)^2-2/(1+b/2)^2
     <=2-4/(1+s/4)^2<=-14/25<0.

This also covers the A=0 base profile. Therefore Jensen applies to the N
color classes, including empty classes, exactly as in the earlier proof.

3. Cauchy-Schwarz step with the profile retained.
Let m be a positive limiting normalized edge count and write

                  f_C(u)=u/(1+u A_C(u))
                        =u(1+Cu)/(1+(C+1)u).

Its second derivative is -2/[1+(C+1)u]^3<0. The two denominator terms in
the Cauchy-Schwarz loss therefore sum to at most

                 f_C(a)+f_C(b)<=s/[1+(s/2)A_C(s/2)].

The endpoint argument consequently gives a new bound with denominator
coefficient 1+R(s,m), where

 R(s,m)=(s-2m)^2/[s/(1+(s/2)A_C(s/2))+m(s-2m)].

As previously, R decreases with m. The assumed bound implies

 m<=s^2/[4+s+s^2 A_C(s)].

Set D=4+s+s^2 A_C(s), B=4-s+s^2 A_C(s). Then

 R(s,m)/s >= Q_C(s)
   := B^2/[D^2/(1+(s/2)A_C(s/2))+s^2 B].                   (2)

We next prove Q_C(s)>=1/(1+K(C)s), uniformly for 0<=s<=1.

4. Exact polynomial certificate for the profile recurrence.
Put

 U=1+Cs, W=2+Cs, V=2+(C+1)s,
 Dn=4+(4C+1)s+(C+1)s^2,
 Bn=4+(4C-1)s+(1-C)s^2.

These are positive for C>=1 and s in [0,1]: in particular Dn=UD and Bn=UB.
Then Q_C(s)=Bn^2 V/[Dn^2 W+s^2 Bn U V].
Write K(C)=KN/KD, where KN and KD are the numerator and denominator in (1).
Exact polynomial expansion gives

 (KD+KN s)Bn^2 V - KD(Dn^2 W+s^2 Bn U V)
       =s(1-s)[q0+q1 s+q2 s^2+q3 s^3+q4 s^4],             (3)

where

 q0=384+1568C+1584C^2+464C^3,
 q1=-384+736C+3984C^2+3872C^3+1088C^4,
 q2=-72-438C+771C^2+3345C^3+2964C^4+784C^5,
 q3=-36-9C+34C^2+341C^3+822C^4+648C^5+160C^6,
 q4=-36-9C+142C^2+116C^3-78C^4-107C^5-28C^6.

For C>=1, q0,q1,q2,q3 and q3+q4 are all positive. Explicitly, q1's first
two terms already sum to at least 352; q2's first three terms are positive
because 771C^2-438C-72 is increasing and positive for C>=1. For q3 use
341C^3-9C-36>0. Finally

 q3+q4=-72-18C+176C^2+457C^3+744C^4+541C^5+132C^6>0,

since its first three terms are already increasing and positive at C=1.
Rewrite the last two terms in (3)'s bracket as

               q3 s^3(1-s)+(q3+q4)s^4.

Thus the right side of (3) is nonnegative. All denominators are positive,
so (3) proves Q_C(s)>=1/(1+K(C)s). Combined with (2), it proves the profile
recurrence (1).

5. Starting from the base profile A=0.
The same calculation before introducing C gives

 Q_0(s)=(4-s)^2/[(4+s)^2+s^2(4-s)].

Here the subscript denotes the zero profile, not A_C at C=0. The identity

 (1+(19/9)s)(4-s)^2 - [(4+s)^2+s^2(4-s)]
                  =(4/9)s(1-s)(40-7s)>=0

shows that Q_0(s)>=1/(1+(19/9)s). Hence the base bound implies the first
profile A_{19/9}, as asserted.

6. Uniform errors and boundary cases.
The uniform compactness proof in section F of recursive-density-bound.txt
applies verbatim. Endpoint decomposition introduces at most twice the prior
uniform normalized error plus O(1/N). Jensen is valid by section 2. If a new
uniform bound failed by eta>0, a convergent subsequence of (a,b,m) would have
m>=eta, forcing a,b,s positive. The limit then obeys sections 3-4, a
contradiction. Thus no division by a vanishing m or part size is concealed.
Each iteration is fixed before N tends to infinity.

7. Limit of the profile parameters.
Let C_0=19/9 and C_{j+1}=K(C_j). The derivative of K is positive: after
multiplying by its squared denominator, its numerator is

                294C^4+1670C^3+3621C^2+3528C+1296.

For C>=1, K(C)>=1 because KN-KD=10C^3+24C^2+5C-12>0.
The sign of C-K(C) is the sign of

                       P(C)=9C^4+32C^3+13C^2-45C-36.

P(1)<0, P(19/9)>0, and P'(C)=36C^3+96C^2+26C-45>0 for C>=1. There is
therefore a unique fixed point C_*>1. Monotonicity of K shows C_j decreases
to C_*, always remaining in [C_*,19/9]. The corresponding profiles increase.
Take N->infinity for each fixed j, then j->infinity. This yields the limiting
profile A_{C_*}, without needing an error estimate uniform in j.

8. Global graph bound.
For the cut between the endpoints of a color matching and all other
vertices, the normalized side total is s=1. Thus only A_{C_*}(1)=alpha is
used in the final global Jensen calculation. The internal endpoint graph
still contributes at most K^2 edges. As in section H of the earlier proof,
the function

             2x^2 + z/[1+(1+alpha)z],  z=2x(1-2x),

is concave because alpha<1/2. Summing endpoint degree inequalities gives
the stated cubic density bound. Properification transfers it unchanged to
arbitrary rainbow-C4 colorings with <=N colors.

The numerical constant describes an exactly specified algebraic number;
the proof does not depend on floating-point root calculations.

9. Exact algebra and interval certificate.
check_profile_polynomial.py uses integer polynomial arithmetic to verify (3)
and rational arithmetic to certify

 1.18541655912282415 < C_* < 1.18541655912282417,
 0.29844403750999554 < rho_profile < 0.29844403750999556.

The resulting exact signed numerators and denominators are saved in
profile-polynomial-certificate.json. The density interval uses conservative
endpoint signs over the entire certified C interval. The normalized cubic
increases with C for a fixed rho in (0,1/2), and decreases with rho, so the
sign certificates bracket its root. This check verifies algebra and root
isolation; it does not replace the combinatorial proof above.

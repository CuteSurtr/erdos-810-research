Four-edge obstruction classification (mathematical proof; converse not formalized)

Let G be a simple bipartite graph with a proper edge coloring. Lift each edge uv
of color k to the triple {u,v,k}, where the vertex and color sets are disjoint.
Then the lifted hypergraph is (7,4)-free if and only if:
  (1) every C4 in G is rainbow; and
  (2) G has no 4-edge path on 5 distinct vertices whose colors alternate p,q,p,q.
Here p and q must be different because the original coloring is proper.

Proof. Fix any four distinct graph edges, let v be the number of their distinct
endpoints and let t be the number of their colors. Their lifted union has exactly
v+t points. The proper coloring restricts to a proper coloring of this four-edge
support graph.

If v+t <= 7 and t=1, properness makes the four edges a matching, giving v=8,
a contradiction. Therefore t>=2 and v<=5. Four distinct edges in a simple
bipartite graph require at least four incident vertices.

If v=4, its bipartition must have sizes 2 and 2: a 1+3 bipartition permits only
three edges. All four possible edges are consequently present, giving a C4.
Since t<=3, that C4 is not rainbow.

If v=5, then t=2. Properness gives maximum degree at most two. Each component of
the support is a path or an even cycle. A cycle would have to use all four edges
as a C4, leaving only four incident vertices, contrary to v=5. Thus the support
is a forest. A forest with five vertices and four edges is connected, so the
support is a single path with five distinct vertices. Its proper two-coloring
must alternate p,q,p,q.

Conversely a non-rainbow C4 has v=4 and t<=3, and an alternating two-color path
on five vertices has v=5 and t=2. Either therefore gives four distinct triples
spanning at most seven points. This proves both directions.

Scope and formal verification:
- This is an elementary structural classification derived in this audit; no
  literature novelty claim is made.
- BipartiteHypergraphExtraction.lean proves the general forward consequences
  from actual global (7,4)-freeness, with finite sets of arbitrary triples.
- ProperBipartiteLift.lean kernel-checks the properly colored, bipartite,
  alternating-P5 counterexample, including exact lift, linearity, and span 7.
- The general sufficiency direction above is supplied as a mathematical proof,
  not claimed to have been checked in Lean.

PASS 6: INDEPENDENT AUDIT OF THE OFF-DIAGONAL OPERATOR NORM
Date: 2026-10-15
Status: PASS. Complete elementary proof, including normalization.

1. Statement

Let p be an odd prime, s in F_p^*, and w:F_p^*->C satisfy |w(a)|<=1.
On functions on F_p define

 C_(s,w) f(x)=sum_(a!=0) w(a) e_p(s*(a^2*x+a^3)) f(a*x+a^2).

Then, for an absolute constant C,

                    ||C_(s,w)||_(2->2) <= C*p^(7/8).       (1)

The norm is unchanged by choosing counting or normalized counting
measure. No L-infinity bound on the input or test function is needed.
The estimate is uniform in the weights, their support, and s!=0.

The exact partial-Fourier/Mellin decomposition audited in Section 5
then proves the same estimate for arbitrary complex |v(a)|<=1 in

 R_(s,v)=sum_(a!=0) v(a) M_[e_p(s*(a^2*x+a^3))] U_(tau_a),
 tau_a(x,y)=(y/a-a, a*x+a^2).

This resolves the individual off-diagonal operator norm left open in
pass5-offdiagonal-chirp-operator-checkpoint.txt. It does not by itself
control the full sum of Fourier slices for arbitrary common H.

2. Two Cauchy-Schwarz steps and the exact pair phase

Write E_x=p^(-1)sum_x, and take E|f|^2=E|g|^2=1. Extend w by w(0)=0.
Set

 T=E_x conj(g(x)) E_a w(a)e_p(s*(a^2*x+a^3)) f(a*x+a^2)
  =p^(-1) E_x conj(g(x)) C_(s,w)f(x).

Cauchy-Schwarz in x, then the triangle inequality and Cauchy-Schwarz
over the p^2 ordered pairs (a,b), give

 |T|^4 <= p^(-2) sum_(a,b!=0) |I_ab|^2,                   (2)

where

 I_ab=E_x e_p(s*(u_a(x)-u_b(x)))
                 f(a*x+a^2) conj(f(b*x+b^2)),
 u_a(x)=a^2*x+a^3.

Put b=t*a and y=a*x+a^2. Then

 b*x+b^2=t*y+a^2*t*(t-1),
 u_a(x)-u_b(x)=a*(1-t^2)*y-a^3*t^2*(t-1).

The last term is constant in y, and disappears under the absolute
value in (2). Thus, writing c=t*(t-1), d=s*(1-t^2),

 |I_ab|=|E_y f(y)conj(f(t*y+c*a^2))e_p(d*a*y)|.           (3)

For t=1 or t=-1, the absolute value in (3) is at most 1, by
Cauchy-Schwarz and the fact that y->t*y+c*a^2 is a permutation.
Their total contribution to (2) is at most 2/p.

3. Fixed t different from 0,1,-1

Fix such a t, so both c and d are nonzero. The nonnegative sum over
a!=0 may be enlarged to the sum over all a. Put

 S_t=sum_a |E_y f(y)conj(f(t*y+c*a^2))e_p(d*a*y)|^2.

Expanding and writing r=y-y' gives

 S_t=p^(-2)sum_r sum_y G_r(y) (L_r H_r)(y),

 G_r(y)=f(y)conj(f(y-r)),
 H_r(z)=conj(f(z))f(z-t*r),
 L_r H(y)=sum_a e_p(d*a*r) H(t*y+c*a^2).

All norms in the rest of this section use counting measure.

For r!=0, L_r sends the normalized Fourier character of frequency xi
to that of frequency t*xi, multiplied by

                 sum_a e_p(c*xi*a^2+d*r*a).

At xi=0 this is zero. At xi!=0 it has absolute value sqrt(p).
For completeness, if alpha!=0, expansion of
|sum_a e_p(alpha*a^2+beta*a)|^2 and the substitution a=b+h give

 sum_h e_p(alpha*h^2+beta*h)sum_b e_p(2*alpha*h*b)=p,

because p is odd and only h=0 survives. Hence ||L_r||=sqrt(p).

Furthermore,

 sum_r ||G_r||_2^2=(sum_y |f(y)|^2)^2=p^2,
 sum_r ||H_r||_2^2=(sum_z |f(z)|^2)^2=p^2,

where the second equality uses t!=0. Cauchy-Schwarz first in y and
then in r bounds the absolute contribution from r!=0 by

 p^(-2)*sqrt(p)*sum_(r!=0)||G_r||_2||H_r||_2 <= sqrt(p).

For r=0, its nonnegative contribution is

 p^(-2)sum_y |f(y)|^2 sum_a |f(t*y+c*a^2)|^2 <= 2.

Indeed, for each fixed y the map a->t*y+c*a^2 has at most two
preimages per value, and sum_z|f(z)|^2=p. Therefore

                         S_t <= 2+sqrt(p).                (4)

4. Summation and norm conclusion

There are p-3 values of t other than 0,1,-1. Equations (2)--(4) give

 |T|^4 <= 2/p + (p-3)*(2+sqrt(p))/p^2 = O(p^(-1/2)).      (5)

Since T=p^(-1)<g,C_(s,w)f> with normalized inner product, taking
the supremum over unit f,g gives (1). Every use of f involved only
its L2 norm or the exact correlation identities above. The proof
does not assume flatness, bounded entries, or an L-infinity norm.

5. Independent check of the full R_s reduction

For a,b!=0 put

 r=a/b, q=(a^2-b^2)/b, t=b*(b-a), c0=s*a^2*(a-b).

The previously saved exact identity is

 R_a R_b^* f(x,y)
   =e_p(-s*r*t*x+c0) f(r*x+q, r^(-1)*y+t).

Expand f(x,y)=sum_eta f_eta(x)e_p(eta*y). The output frequency eta
uses input frequency r*eta, and the coefficient is

 e_p(-s*r*t*x+r*eta*t+c0) f_(r*eta)(r*x+q).

The unitary, eta-dependent shift g_eta(z)=f_eta(z+eta/s) changes this to

                  e_p(-s*r*t*z+c0) g_(r*eta)(r*z+q).

On eta=0 this is the trivial-character block. On eta!=0, decompose
by the full orthonormal multiplicative-character basis. For a block
g_eta(z)=chi(eta)g(z), the additional factor is

                       chi(r)=chi(a)conj(chi(b)).

For arbitrary complex weights v(a), the pair product also contains
v(a)conj(v(b)). The block of R_(s,v) R_(s,v)^* is therefore
C_(s,chi*v)C_(s,chi*v)^*.
The trivial character also occurs in the eta=0 block. These blocks
exhaust the whole space, so

                    ||R_(s,v)||=max_chi ||C_(s,chi*v)||.

Applying (1) gives ||R_(s,v)||=O(p^(7/8)), uniformly in |v|<=1
and s!=0. Taking v=1_A recovers R_s from the previous checkpoint.

6. Scope of this improvement

For the common-potential masks 1_H(a^2*x+a^3), the full operator
has coefficients hhat(s-l)*hhat(l) multiplying chirp conjugates of
R_s. A uniform estimate for each R_s does not justify dropping or
freely summing these coefficients. Their absolute total can grow
with p. A separate argument is still needed for arbitrary H; this
audit makes no sparsity claim for that full class.

No finite enumeration or numerical matrix calculations were used.

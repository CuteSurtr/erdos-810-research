FIXED-DEGREE NONLINEAR POLYNOMIAL INTERCEPTS: COMMON-SUPPORT POWER SAVING
15 October 2026. Complete analytic proof, independently audited.

Status: a restricted-class theorem for proper B colorings. This is not a
solution of unrestricted Erdos #810. The proof uses the classical Weil
bound for prime-field Kloosterman sums. It is not Lean certified, and
historical priority of this formulation has not been established.

1. THEOREM

Let p be an odd prime and beta in F_p[T] have degree D with 2<=D<p.
Let A subset F_p* have size d. For each a in A choose D_a subset X
subset F_p, and select the edges

                   x -> sigma_a(x)=ax+beta(a), x in D_a,

with color a. Assume the selected graph is simple and properly colored,
and every C4 is rainbow (condition B). Set

                  m=sum_a |D_a|, R=sum_a (|X|-|D_a|).

For each fixed D there is C_D independent of p,beta,A,X such that

                 m <= C_D p^(19/10)+sqrt(2) p sqrt(R).       (1)

Consequently common support gives O_D(p^(19/10)) edges, and R=o(p^2)
gives m=o(p^2) for bounded D. Common support and d>=delta p give the
sharper O_{D,delta}(p^(15/8)) estimate. Rectangular support covers and
approximate color groups obey the same consequences as in the quadratic
note, with constants depending on D.

The exclusion of degree at most one is essential to this power-saving
argument. Such intercepts give concurrent affine pencils. The theorem
does not assume additive or multiplicative structure of A, and beta may
vary with p subject to the fixed degree bound.

2. CROSSED INVOLUTIONS: THE COMBINATORIAL ESTIMATE IS UNCHANGED

On V=F_p^2 define

 tau_a(x,y)=((y-beta(a))/a, ax+beta(a)),
 S_A f=sum_{a in A} f composed with tau_a.

These are involutions, so S_A is self-adjoint of constant degree d.
For E the selected edge set, an internal transition is an ordered triple
(a,x,z) with (x,sigma_a(z)) and (z,sigma_a(x)) both in E. If the two
diagonal color-a incidences at x,z are present, B forces x=z. The actual
loops contribute exactly m. Every other transition has a missing
diagonal incidence, including any virtual loop whose point is present
in another color. Each missing (a,x) occurs in at most deg_E(x)<=d
transitions in either position. Therefore

                  <1_E,S_A1_E> <= m+2dR.                     (2)

For R=0 equality holds. This proof uses only that sigma_a is a
permutation and the actual graph is simple, proper and B-colored.

3. GENERAL FOURIER BLOCK AND ITS KLOOSTERMAN FOURTH MOMENT

For chi_(xi,eta)(x,y)=e_p(xi x+eta y),

 P_tau_a chi_(xi,eta)
   =e_p(beta(a)(eta-xi/a)) chi_(a eta,xi/a).

The frequency product xi eta is invariant. In a nonzero product block
t, indexed by (r,t/r), the matrix is

 M_t(r,s)=1_A(rs/t) e_p((r-s) beta(rs/t)/(rs/t)).              (3)

It is Hermitian. Write F(a)=beta(a)/a. Expand tr M_t^4 over r,s,u,v,
and make the bijective change

 a=rs/t, q=u/r, h=v/s, s=ta/r, u=qr, v=hta/r.

The four membership tests are a,qa,qha,ha in A. The phase is
alpha r+gamma/r, where

 alpha=[h beta(a)-h beta(qa)+beta(qha)-beta(ha)]/(ha),
 gamma=(t/q)[-q beta(a)+beta(qa)-beta(qha)+q beta(ha)].         (4)

One direct check is that the coefficient of r before substituting
s=ta/r is F(a)-qF(qa)+qF(qha)-F(ha), and that of s is
-F(a)+F(qa)-hF(qha)+hF(ha).

If (alpha,gamma)!=(0,0), the sum over r!=0 has absolute value at most
2sqrt(p): this is Weil's Kloosterman estimate when both coefficients
are nonzero, and additive-character orthogonality gives -1 when exactly
one is nonzero. If both vanish, the sum is p-1.

4. ALL EXCEPTIONAL TRIPLES ARE CONTROLLED BY COLLINEARITY

The cases q=1 or h=1 are automatically exceptional and account for
2d^2-d supported triples. Suppose now q,h!=1. The two coefficient rows
in (4), on the value vector (beta(a),beta(qa),beta(qha),beta(ha)), are

                  [ h,-h, 1,-1], [-q,1,-1,q].

They are linearly independent. Indeed comparison at coordinate 3 in a
putative dependence forces multiplier -1, and coordinates 2 and 1 would
then force h=q=1. Both rows annihilate the independent vectors

                   (1,1,1,1), (a,qa,qha,ha).

The latter are independent because q!=1. Thus their common kernel is
exactly the vectors of affine-in-slope values. In particular, an
exceptional triple makes all four graph points

 (a,beta(a)), (qa,beta(qa)), (qha,beta(qha)), (ha,beta(ha))

collinear. This proof includes coincident slopes, such as q=h=-1;
it never presumes four distinct points.

Choose a and b=qa as an ordered distinct pair from A. Their affine line
intersects the graph of beta in at most D points, because beta(T) minus
that line's equation is a nonzero degree-D polynomial. Thus c=ha has at
most D choices, and the fourth slope bc/a is then determined. Hence

 exceptional triples <=2d^2-d+Dd(d-1) <=(D+2)d^2.            (5)

The total supported triples are the multiplicative energy
E_x(A)=#{(a,b,c,e) in A^4: ae=bc}<=d^3. Therefore

              tr M_t^4 <=(D+2)p d^2+2sqrt(p) E_x(A).        (6)

5. THE AXIS BLOCK

The nonzero coordinate axes form an off-diagonal block whose norm is
the norm on mean-zero functions of the one-point affine operator
C_A=sum_a P_sigma_a. Its Fourier matrix, up to taking an adjoint, is

                 N(r,s)=1_A(r/s)e_p(s beta(r/s)).

For an affine quotient sigma_a sigma_b^-1 put rho=a/b. Its slope and
intercept are

                       rho, beta(rho b)-rho beta(b).        (7)

If rho^(D-1)!=1, the second expression is a degree-D polynomial in b
with nonzero leading coefficient. Every quotient with that slope has
at most D representations. The contribution to quotient energy from
all such slopes is therefore at most Dd^2.

There are at most D-1 remaining ratios rho. For each one, the total
number of pairs (a,b) in A^2 with a/b=rho is at most d, so the sum of
squared quotient multiplicities for that ratio is at most d^2. Thus
the affine quotient energy satisfies

 E_aff(A):=#{(a,b,c,e) in A^4:sigma_a sigma_b^-1
                                  =sigma_c sigma_e^-1}
                         <=(2D-1)d^2.                       (8)

The character of the mean-zero permutation representation of Aff(F_p)
is p-1 at identity, -1 at a nontrivial translation, and zero elsewhere.
Expanding tr((C_A C_A*)^2) and dropping the negative translation terms
proves

                     ||N||^4 <=(p-1) E_aff(A).              (9)

Taking inverses if necessary to use a genuine representation rather
than a pullback antirepresentation changes neither these norms nor
the energy count.

6. FINISHING THE BOUND

Let K_D=max(D+2,2D-1). All nonconstant Fourier blocks are bounded by

 lambda=[K_D p d^2+2sqrt(p) E_x(A)]^(1/4)
       <=C_D[p^(1/4)d^(1/2)+p^(1/8)d^(3/4)].                (10)

Centering 1_E in the p^2-point space and using (2) gives

             (d+lambda)m^2/p^2 <=(1+lambda)m+2dR.           (11)

For d>0 this implies

 m <=p^2(1+lambda)/d+sqrt(2)p sqrt(R)
   <=p^2/d+C_D p^(9/4)d^(-1/2)
          +C_D p^(17/8)d^(-1/4)+sqrt(2)p sqrt(R).           (12)

If d<=p^(9/10), use m<=dp. Otherwise the three first terms in (12)
are O_D(p^(19/10)). The case d=0 is empty. This proves (1).

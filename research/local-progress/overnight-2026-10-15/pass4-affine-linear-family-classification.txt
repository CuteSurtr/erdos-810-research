ALL NONDEGENERATE AFFINE-LINEAR TWO-PARAMETER ROOT FAMILIES
15 October 2026, overnight pass 4. Complete algebraic proof.

Status: theorem proved below and independently checked by the formal-
audit agent. This classifies one precise type of recurrence family;
it does not prove common-mask sparsity or Erdős #810. Historical
novelty is unverified.

1. STATEMENT

Let k have characteristic different from 2. Work over an algebraic
closure when discussing algebraic sets. Let V be the seven-coordinate
variety with ordered-root slots

  (a,b), (a,c), (d,e), (f,g)

and equations

  a+b=d+e,   ac=de,   a+c=f+g,   ab=fg.             (1)

Let L be an affine 2-plane contained in V. Assume that none of
a,d,f,b-c is identically zero on L. Thus its generic points have
valid nonzero color coordinates and describe nondegenerate repeated-
color rectangles.

THEOREM. L is one of exactly four linear planes. Every one satisfies

  b+c+2a=0.                                          (2)

Using the free parameters (a,b), put c=-2a-b. The four planes are
obtained by the two independent root-order choices

  (d,e) = (-a,2a+b) or (2a+b,-a),
  (f,g) = (-a,-b)   or (-b,-a).                      (3)

There are no additional affine translates. There is also no
nondegenerate affine-linear family of dimension greater than 2.

The graph rows of the two diagonal edges are

  x=-(a+b),       z=-(a+c)=a+b=-x.                   (4)

Thus EVERY such two-parameter affine-linear root family has opposite
rows. The choice d=2a+b, f=-b has d+f=2a and gives the familiar
arithmetic-progression color template. Each of the other three
choices has an antipodal color -a.

2. THE GENERIC DETERMINANT FORMULAS

Set Delta=df-a^2. From (1), b,c satisfy

  db-ac=d(d-a),      ab-fc=f(a-f).                   (5)

When Delta!=0, the other coordinates are uniquely determined by a,d,f:

  P=d^2-ad+af-a^2,       Q=f^2-af+ad-a^2,
  b=fP/Delta,           c=dQ/Delta,
  e=aQ/Delta,           g=aP/Delta.

In particular,

  Delta(b-c)=(d-f)(d-a)(f-a).                        (6)

These formulas and their exceptional cases were independently
established in pass3-quadratic-variety-and-cubic-phase-obstruction.txt.

Suppose first that Delta is not identically zero on L. Projection
onto (a,d,f) is injective on the open subset Delta!=0, because (5)
has a unique solution and e,g are then determined. Since projection
is an affine-linear map on L, it has zero kernel and identifies L
with an affine 2-plane H in the three-dimensional (a,d,f) space.
In particular b,c,e,g are affine-linear functions on H.

3. THE PROJECTED PLANE MUST PASS THROUGH THE ORIGIN

Write Q_0(a,d,f)=df-a^2. This is a nondegenerate quadratic form in
three variables when char(k)!=2. Its restriction to the direction
space of an affine 2-plane cannot vanish identically: otherwise
that two-dimensional direction space would be totally isotropic,
contained in its polar orthogonal complement of dimension one.
Thus Q_0 restricted to H has degree exactly two.

Equation (6), in the polynomial ring k[H], says that Q_0|H divides
the product of the three affine-linear functions d-f,d-a,f-a.
None of those functions is identically zero, since otherwise (6)
would force b=c. Consequently Q_0|H factors into two affine-linear
factors, allowing repetition.

Each resulting factor line in H lies in the cone Q_0=0 and in at
least one of the three planes d=f,d=a,f=a. Those intersections are
unions of only the following four lines through the origin:

  ell_0 = {(t,t,t)},
  ell_1 = {(0,0,t)},
  ell_2 = {(0,t,0)},
  ell_3 = {(t,-t,-t)}.                               (7)

For example Q_0|_{d=a}=a(f-a), and Q_0|_{d=f}=d^2-a^2.
An affine line contained in the union of these finitely many lines
must equal one of them. Thus H contains an entire line through the
origin, and hence contains the origin itself. H is a linear plane.

4. SECANT AND TANGENT CASES

If Q_0|H has two distinct factor lines, H is the span of two distinct
lines from (7). There are six possibilities:

  span(ell_0,ell_1): d=a, forcing b=c;
  span(ell_0,ell_2): f=a, forcing b=c;
  span(ell_0,ell_3): d=f, forcing b=c;
  span(ell_1,ell_2): a=0, inadmissible;
  span(ell_1,ell_3): d=-a;
  span(ell_2,ell_3): f=-a.

Only the last two are possible nondegenerate families.

If Q_0|H has a double factor line ell_i, then H is the tangent plane
to Q_0 at a nonzero point of that line. One can see this by polarizing:
the derivative of the square restriction vanishes at every point
of ell_i, so the polar form with that point vanishes on all H.
The four tangent planes are

  at ell_0: d+f=2a;
  at ell_1: d=0, inadmissible;
  at ell_2: f=0, inadmissible;
  at ell_3: d+f=-2a.

The last one cannot satisfy (6). On it,

  Delta=-(a+d)^2,
  (d-f)(d-a)(f-a)=-2(a+d)(d-a)(d+3a).

The numerator has only one factor a+d: neither d-a nor d+3a
vanishes identically on d=-a in odd characteristic. Therefore
Delta cannot divide the numerator. This also checks characteristic
3 explicitly; the only excluded characteristic is 2.

We are left with exactly three generic projected planes:

  d=-a,    f=-a,    d+f=2a.                          (8)

Substitution in the determinant formulas gives b+c=-2a in all three.
For clarity:

  d=-a, f=t: b=-t, c=t-2a, e=2a-t, g=-a;
  f=-a, d=t: b=t-2a, c=-t, e=-a, g=2a-t;
  d=a+h, f=a-h: b=h-a, c=-h-a, e=g=-a.

5. THE EXCEPTIONAL DETERMINANT-ZERO CASE

Suppose instead that Delta is identically zero on L. Work in the
function field of L, where a,d,f are nonzero. Write d=ar, f=a/r.
Consistency of (5) gives

  (r-1)^2(r+1)=0.

Since the coordinate ring is a domain, either d=f=a identically,
or d=f=-a identically. The first case forces b=c and is excluded.
The second forces

  d=f=-a,   b+c=-2a,   e=-c,   g=-b.                 (9)

These equations define a linear 2-plane. Since L also has dimension
two and is contained in it, L equals that plane. This supplies the
fourth family and completes the classification.

6. THE HYPERPLANE SECTION AND MAXIMAL DIMENSION

Conversely, impose (2) in (1). The roots in the third slot have
sum a+b and product -a(2a+b), so they are -a and 2a+b. Those in
the fourth slot have sum -a-b and product ab, so they are -a,-b.
This gives exactly (3). Thus V intersect {b+c+2a=0} is, set-theoretically,
the union of precisely the four displayed linear planes.

If a nondegenerate affine-linear space of dimension at least three
were contained in V, generic affine 2-planes inside it would satisfy
the theorem. They would all lie in b+c+2a=0; consequently the whole
space would lie in that hyperplane. An irreducible affine space
contained in a finite union of four 2-planes is contained in one of
them, which contradicts its larger dimension.

7. FINITE-FIELD INTERPRETATION

The statement is about formal algebraic parameter families, and so
avoids an inference from sparse finite-field samples. In particular
it applies whenever all seven roots are affine-linear in two formal
parameters and (1) holds as polynomial identities.

If p>=7, a,d,f,b-c are each nonzero as affine functions, and an
injective affine parametrization over F_p satisfies (1) for EVERY
parameter pair for which a,d,f,b-c are all nonzero,
the same conclusion follows. The four inadmissible affine equations
remove at most 4p parameter pairs. Each relation in (1) has degree
at most two; if it were a nonzero polynomial, it would vanish at
at most 2p pairs. Since p^2-4p>2p for p>=7, all relations must be
polynomial identities. The classification then applies after base
extension to the algebraic closure.

8. CONSEQUENCE AND EXACT LIMIT FOR THE COMMON-MASK TARGET

Suppose all actual color supports are the same row domain X. If
X intersect (-X) is empty, no nondegenerate configuration lying in
ANY of the classified affine 2-planes can occur, regardless of the
palette. Thus a recurrence argument whose seven root coordinates
are all affine-linear in two independent parameters sees only a
pattern that an antisymmetric row domain can exclude completely.

This is a meaningful obstruction because proper dense common-mask
graphs with antisymmetric X exist. For example, for primes p>240,
take A=X={1,...,floor(p/10)} and all edges y=ax+a^2, a in A,x in X.
They are proper: an intersection would require x+a+b=0 mod p for
distinct a,b in A, whereas this positive integer is less than p.
Also X intersect (-X) is empty and the edge density tends to 1/100.

This graph is NOT B. It contains the explicit rectangle

  rows 9,24; columns 52,112; colors

       4   7
       2   4.

Its root coordinates are

  (a,b,c,d,e,f,g)=(4,-13,-28,7,-16,2,-26),

which satisfy (1) but have b+c+2a=-33!=0 mod p. Therefore this
actual forbidden rectangle lies on none of the nondegenerate
affine 2-planes. The general three-dimensional rational family
contains configurations beyond all of the linear recurrence families.

No claim is made that every possible use of a linear recurrence
theorem is ruled out. Nonlinear changes of variables, parameter
families lying on curved surfaces, affine one-parameter families,
or arguments combining several families are outside this theorem.
In particular, this classification does not settle common-mask
sparsity; it identifies why the most direct two-parameter affine
root recurrences cannot settle it.

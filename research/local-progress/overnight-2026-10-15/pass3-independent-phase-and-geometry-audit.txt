INDEPENDENT AUDIT: CUBIC PHASES, DENSE FOUR-PARTITE SETS, AND GEOMETRY
15 October 2026. All checks below are algebraic hand proofs.
No finite search, new asymptotic edge bound, or novelty claim.

1. CUBIC PHASE NOTE: PASS

Audited in full:
  pass3-quadratic-variety-and-cubic-phase-obstruction.txt

The rational parametrization, determinant-zero families, exact total
count (p-1)(p^2-p+2), and exact nondegenerate count
(p-1)(p^2-5p+8) all check. An independent degeneracy count is

  4(p-1)(p-2)+2(p-1)=(p-1)(4p-6),

which equals the difference between the two displayed counts.

The cubic identity follows directly from

 de(d+e)=ac(a+b)=a^2c+abc,
 fg(f+g)=ab(a+c)=a^2b+abc.

All conditional-expectation normalizations check. In particular,
color fibers have p points, sum and product fibers have p-1 points,
and the latter factors are uniform on F_p. For e_p(lambda r^2u),
the product-marginal squared norm is exactly 1/(p-1). For
e_p(lambda ru(r+u)), the product-marginal expansion has (p-1)
diagonal terms of size p and (p-1)(p-2) off-diagonal quadratic sums
of modulus sqrt(p); division by p(p-1)^2 gives exactly the stated
upper bound

        1/(p-1)+(p-2)/[(p-1)sqrt(p)].

Thus all conditional L2 norms are o(1), uniformly in every nonzero
frequency lambda. The weakest bound is O(p^(-1/4)). The sum-fiber
estimate for r^2u uses only r^2=v^2 after orthogonality, and the
other estimates require only the elementary quadratic Gauss-sum
modulus. No unproved Kloosterman or higher-degree estimate enters.

2. THE FOUR DENSE INDICATOR SETS: PASS, WITH THEIR STATED SCOPE

Let J_p consist of standard residues strictly between p/16 and
3p/16. Select in slot i exactly those pairs whose signed phase
polynomial lies in J_p. On a solution, the four signed polynomials
sum to zero modulo p. But four selected standard residues have
ordinary integer sum strictly between p/4 and 3p/4. This is
impossible, so there are no configurations at all.

The normalized additive Fourier coefficients of an interval have
l1 norm O(log p). Expanding its indicator, applying the uniform
nonzero-frequency conditional L2 estimates, and using the triangle
inequality gives an error O(p^(-1/4)log p)=o(1) for each color, sum,
and product marginal. The global density also tends to 1/8 by
Cauchy--Schwarz (or by taking the expectation of any marginal).

These four supports are different and need not be properly oriented.
This refutes a proposed four-partite counting/supersaturation theorem
based solely on these marginal conditions. It does not refute a
removal theorem: a zero-solution example already needs no deletions.
It also does not refute the common properly oriented support problem.

3. DIFFERENTIAL LINEARIZATION OBSTRUCTION: PASS ON THE CORRECT OPEN SET

The following calculation independently checks the candidate written
by the literature agent in
  pass3-four-projection-linearization-obstruction.txt.

In U=a,V=a+b,W=ac coordinates, with U!=0, the four slot-coordinate
maps can be expressed as

  (U,V-U),   (U,W/U),   (V,W),
  (S,T)=(U+W/U, U(V-U)).

Their kernel line fields are respectively

  partial_W, partial_V, partial_U,
  X=partial_U+(2-V/U)partial_V+(W/U-U)partial_W.

For the last map, this follows immediately from

  dS=(1-W/U^2)dU+dW/U,
  dT=(V-2U)dU+U dV.

The bracket with partial_U is

  [partial_U,X]=(V/U^2)partial_V+(-W/U^2-1)partial_W.

Taking these three vector fields as columns gives

 det(partial_U,X,[partial_U,X])
   = -2(U^2-UV+W)/U^2
   = -2(c-b)/a.

Therefore, in odd characteristic, at every b!=c point the span of
the third and fourth kernel lines is not closed under brackets.
For constant kernel lines of affine-linear rank-two maps, their
span is closed under brackets. Invertible differentiable coordinate
changes preserve brackets and this closure property; invertible
output relabelings preserve the kernel distribution. Hence these
four maps cannot be simultaneously changed into affine-linear maps
by such coordinate and separate output changes near a genuine point.

The third and fourth maps here use sum/product coordinates. To apply
the same statement to selected ORDERED root-pair slots, the change
(d,e)->(d+e,de) is locally invertible only when d!=e, and similarly
the fourth slot requires f!=g. Thus for ordered root maps one must
restrict to this additional generic open set. That is enough for
an obstruction to a birational simultaneous linearization.

This argument is about local transformations with invertible
differential (or birational transformations on their nonsingular
open sets). It does not address arbitrary relabelings of finite
sets, inseparable transformations, larger auxiliary spaces, or
hypergraph representations.

4. COMBINED INTERPRETATION

The phase obstruction shows that natural one-coordinate marginals
miss an exact cubic compatibility. The geometric obstruction shows
that the coupling cannot be eliminated by a nonsingular change to
an affine-linear three-variable system. Neither supplies the
uniform inverse or removal theorem still required for quadratic
affine B-sparsity. The separate polynomial classification in
pass3-all-polynomial-slot-potentials.txt is also only an algebraic
classification of formal identities, not an analytic inverse theorem.

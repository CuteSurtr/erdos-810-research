AN OBSTRUCTION TO SIMULTANEOUS LOCAL LINEARIZATION OF THE FOUR SLOT MAPS
15 October 2026.

Status: elementary differential-algebra calculation checked by the root
agent and independently passed in
pass3-independent-phase-and-geometry-audit.txt. This is a
representation obstruction, not a density bound or a proof of the
four-slot removal conjecture. No priority claim is made.

1. THE FOUR PROJECTIONS

Work over a field of characteristic different from two. Initially one
may work over R or C with smooth/analytic local coordinate changes; the
calculation is also an identity in a rational function field.

Let U!=0 and define the four maps to two-dimensional coefficient spaces:

    pi_1(U,V,W) = (U,V-U),
    pi_2(U,V,W) = (U,W/U),
    pi_3(U,V,W) = (V,W),
    pi_4(U,V,W) = (U+W/U, U(V-U)).

These are the four marked-support projections of the quadratic relation
after writing U=a, V=a+b, W=ac. For slots 3 and 4, the displayed pair is
the sum and product of the two roots representing the original label.
Every map has differential of rank two on U!=0. For pi_4 this follows
already from the V and W derivative columns, whose determinant is -1.

Claim. There is no invertible local input-coordinate change, together
with separate invertible local changes of the two output coordinates
of each pi_i, that makes all four resulting maps affine-linear of rank
two on any open set containing a point with U^2-UV+W != 0.

In fact the obstruction already comes from pi_3 and pi_4.

2. THE KERNEL LINE FIELDS

Write e_U=d/dU, e_V=d/dV, e_W=d/dW. The kernels of the first three
differentials are, respectively,

    L_1=span(e_W), L_2=span(e_V), L_3=span(e_U).

For pi_4, differentiating its two components gives

    d(U+W/U) = (1-W/U^2)dU + (1/U)dW,
    d(U(V-U)) = (V-2U)dU + U dV.

Consequently its kernel is L_4=span(X), where

    X = e_U + (2-V/U)e_V + (W/U-U)e_W.

Indeed both displayed differentials annihilate X.

3. THE NON-INTEGRABLE PAIR

The bracket is

    [e_U,X] = (V/U^2)e_V + (-W/U^2-1)e_W.

Relative to the ordered frame (e_U,e_V,e_W),

    det(e_U, X, [e_U,X])
      = (2-V/U)(-W/U^2-1) - (W/U-U)(V/U^2)
      = -2 + 2V/U - 2W/U^2
      = -2(U^2-UV+W)/U^2.                         (3.1)

When this is nonzero, e_U and X span a two-dimensional distribution
D=L_3+L_4, but their bracket is not a section of D. Thus D is not
involutive. Over R or C it is not an integrable plane distribution by
the necessary direction of the Frobenius theorem.

In original parameters the determinant is

    -2(a^2-a(a+b)+ac)/a^2 = -2(c-b)/a.

It is therefore nonzero precisely at a!=0, b!=c in odd characteristic.
The same condition b!=c makes the original rectangle nondegenerate.
At b=c the determinant vanishes at that point; this does not assert
integrability on a neighborhood of that hypersurface.

4. WHY THIS RULES OUT THE STATED COORDINATE CHANGES

The differential kernel of a rank-two affine-linear map on a
three-dimensional affine space is a constant line. The sum of two
distinct constant lines is a constant plane distribution, which is
involutive. If the two lines coincide, their sum has dimension one;
that cannot be the image of our two independent lines at a point where
(3.1) is nonzero.

An invertible change of input coordinates carries tangent vector fields
and their brackets to the corresponding transformed fields. It hence
preserves whether the sum of two kernel line distributions is
involutive. Separate changes of output coordinates with invertible
differentials do not change the differential kernels at all: composing
D pi_i with an invertible 2-by-2 matrix leaves its kernel unchanged.

The non-involutive pair L_3+L_4 cannot consequently become the pair of
kernels of two rank-two affine-linear maps. This proves the claim.

The argument is insensitive to permuting the four slots. One
non-involutive pair remains non-involutive under any permutation.

5. ALGEBRAIC AND ROOT-LABEL VERSIONS

All formulas are rational identities with the sole displayed
denominator a power of U. Algebraically, use closure under Lie bracket
of the corresponding rank-two submodule of derivations instead of
analytic integrability. Isomorphisms on open algebraic subsets preserve
that property. Therefore no birational input-coordinate change with
birational separate output-coordinate changes can simultaneously make
these four maps affine-linear on a dense open set in odd
characteristic. A birational coordinate change restricts to an
isomorphism on suitable nonempty open sets; no inference about
arbitrary inseparable maps is needed.

Passing between ordered roots (d,e) and their coefficients
(d+e,de) has Jacobian d-e. Away from repeated roots it is locally
invertible in the analytic setting, or etale in the algebraic setting.
Thus allowing a local choice of ordered roots does not remove the
obstruction on the generic unramified locus: the same kernels pull
back, and the nonzero bracket class remains nonzero. The conclusion
does not rely on making a global rational choice of square roots.

6. WEB-GEOMETRY COMPARISON AND LIMITATIONS

On the further generic locus V!=2U and W!=U^2, these four line fields
are in general position. Five of the six pairs are involutive:

    the three pairs among e_U,e_V,e_W commute;
    [e_V,X]=-(1/U)e_V;
    [e_W,X]=(1/U)e_W.

The sixth is not involutive by (3.1). Thus the integrable-pair count is
five, while it is six for four constant line fields in general
position.

This is the elementary invariant discussed in Dufour--Lehmann,
"Etude des (n+1)-tissus de courbes en dimension n",
Comptes Rendus Mathematique 361 (2023), 1491--1497, p.1493:
    https://www.numdam.org/item/10.5802/crmath.500.pdf
Their main rank theorem concerns abelian 2-form relations. It is NOT
being used as a theorem classifying the scalar polynomial identity
G(a+b,ac)-G(a+c,ab)=H(a,c)-H(a,b).

The result excludes one concrete strategy: an invertible local
three-parameter change of coordinates, with invertible separate
relabelings of the four two-coordinate slots, turning the relation
into a system of affine-linear pair projections.

It does NOT exclude:
  * arbitrary bijections of the finite sets, with no algebraic or
    differential regularity;
  * a higher-dimensional representation, covers with extra variables,
    or a combinatorial graph/hypergraph encoding;
  * a direct nonlinear Fourier, polynomial, recurrence, removal, or
    supersaturation argument;
  * a different mathematical model that is not obtained by the
    invertible coordinate changes specified above.

In particular it does not exclude a nonabelian group representation.
For example, in the solvable Lie algebra with basis A,B,C and brackets
[A,B]=B, [A,C]=-C, [B,C]=0, the four left-invariant lines generated by
A,B,C,A+B+C also have exactly five integrable pairs. The exceptional
pair is span(A,A+B+C), since [A,A+B+C]=B-C is outside that span in
characteristic different from two. Constant coordinate directions
and left-invariant directions on a nonabelian group are different
notions; only the former were excluded above.

This calculation therefore neither disproves the sought removal lemma
nor gives a dense counterexample. It explains why merely replacing the
four nonlinear projections by standard linear square/corner
projections through such coordinate changes cannot work.

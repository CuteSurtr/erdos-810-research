NEXT TARGET ONLY: A SHARED QUADRATIC-POTENTIAL MASK
15 October 2026, pass 5 checkpoint. Algebra audited; transfer UNPROVED.

This note verifies an exact decomposition for a proposed next research
target. It does not claim a new sparsity theorem or a small error estimate
for the terms identified below as unresolved.

1. SETUP AND EXACT IDENTITY

For a in A subset F_p* set

 sigma_a(x)=ax+a^2,
 tau_a(x,y)=((y-a^2)/a,ax+a^2),
 u_a(x)=a^2x+a^3=a sigma_a(x), Q(x,y)=xy.

Write z=(y-a^2)/a. Direct substitution gives

 u_a(z)=ay,
 Q(tau_a(x,y))-Q(x,y)=ay-a^2x-a^3=u_a(z)-u_a(x).            (1)

Both the signs and the constant term in (1) matter.

Take a common set H subset F_p, h=1_H, and actual domains

                         D_a={x:u_a(x) in H}.

Each u_a is an affine permutation, so |D_a|=|H|. If the resulting
selected graph is simple, its edge count is m=d|H|, d=|A|. Properness
and B are additional hypotheses, not automatic consequences of this
domain formula.

2. FOURIER EXPANSION AND THE EXACT GAUGE-DIAGONAL SLICE

Use normalized Fourier coefficients hhat(k)=p^-1 sum_t h(t)e_p(-kt).
The masked crossed operator is

 T_D=sum_(k,l) hhat(k)hhat(l)
            sum_a M_[e_p(k u_a(x)+l u_a(z))] U_tau_a.        (2)

Here the multiplier is evaluated at (x,y), with z=sigma_a^-1(y).
Set C_l=M_[e_p(l Q)] and S_A=sum_a U_tau_a. Since h is real,
hhat(-l)=conj(hhat(l)). Equation (1) shows that the k+l=0 terms in (2)
are precisely

                 T_diag=sum_l |hhat(l)|^2 C_l* S_A C_l.     (3)

If P is the constant projection on F_p^2, the constant parts of (3)
form the positive semidefinite operator

 B_0=d sum_l |hhat(l)|^2 C_l* P C_l
    =(d/p^2)sum_l |hhat(l)|^2
                  |e_p(-lQ)><e_p(-lQ)|.                    (4)

The conjugation signs in (3)--(4) are correct: C_l* U_tau_a C_l
multiplies the crossed transition by e_p(l(Q(tau_a v)-Q(v))).

The completed quadratic operator has a previously audited mean-zero
norm at most lambda, so Parseval gives a valid bound for THIS slice:

            ||T_diag-B_0||<=lambda sum_l |hhat(l)|^2
                            =lambda |H|/p.                 (5)

This statement does not control T_D-T_diag.

3. WHAT REMAINS OUTSIDE THE ESTABLISHED WEIGHTED ESTIMATE

Put s=k+l and define

                 V_s=sum_a M_[e_p(s u_a(x))] U_tau_a.

The full expansion can be rewritten exactly as

          T_D=sum_(s,l) hhat(s-l)hhat(l) C_l* V_s C_l.       (6)

For s=0, V_s=S_A and (6) gives (3). For s!=0 the extra multiplier
e_p(s(a^2x+a^3)) depends on BOTH the color and the row. Therefore the
color-only weighted crossed-operator bound proved earlier does not apply
to V_s as stated. Its Fourier action also mixes the former product blocks:
apart from its scalar phase it sends (xi,eta) to

                           (a eta+s a^2, xi/a).

The product becomes xi eta+s a xi, rather than remaining xi eta.

The full remaining slice is self-adjoint after pairing terms, but it is
not known to be positive. Its coefficients need not be nonnegative.
Consequently it cannot be discarded in a lower bound, and the positive
main term (4) alone gives no sparsity conclusion.

4. A PRECISE CONDITIONAL CANCELLATION TARGET

If a positive-density B example existed in this class, put eta=|H|/p.
Then m=d eta p, with both d/p and eta bounded below. The exact B identity
still gives <1_E,T_D1_E>=m. The l=0 term of (4), and (5), imply

 <1_E,T_diag1_E> >=d eta^2 m^2/p^2-lambda eta m.

For quadratic palettes lambda=O(p^(7/8)). Under the stated hypothetical
density, the first term is Omega(p^3) and the error is o(p^3). Hence the
remaining slice would have to satisfy

                       <1_E,(T_D-T_diag)1_E><=-c p^3

for some fixed c>0 along that hypothetical sequence. Excluding such
negative cancellation using the actual supports and B would settle this
particular mask class. No exclusion is proved here.

The trivial norm bounds give only ||T_D||<=d and
||T_diag||<=d eta, so their difference can be of order d. They supply
no required o(p) control in the dense regime. No Parseval argument or
completed-map mixing estimate presently closes this gap.

AUDIT CONCLUSION. Equations (1)--(6), the positive rank-one slice and
the slice-specific error (5) are sound. The off-diagonal slice is the
explicit unproved target. This checkpoint must not be reported as an
established new edge bound.

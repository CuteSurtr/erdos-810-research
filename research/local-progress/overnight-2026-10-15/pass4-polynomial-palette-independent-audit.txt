INDEPENDENT AUDIT: FIXED-DEGREE NONLINEAR POLYNOMIAL PALETTES
2026-10-15, finite_audit agent. Status: PASS.

This checks formal_audit's extension of root's crossed-involution proof.
The canonical full extension is saved in
pass4-polynomial-intercept-common-support-theorem.txt; this file isolates
the two new algebraic points. It does not claim historical novelty.

Let beta in F_p[T] have degree 2<=D<p, p odd. Use color permutations
sigma_a(x)=a*x+beta(a), a in A subset F_p^*, and the crossed involutions

       tau_a(x,y)=((y-beta(a))/a, a*x+beta(a)).

For common row domains, the exact support argument is unchanged: every
nontrivial internal transition produces an actual C4 with repeated color a,
and each actual edge has exactly one fixed-color loop under properness.

1. NONZERO FOURIER BLOCKS

On the xi*eta=t!=0 block, the Hermitian entry is

 M_t(r,s)=1_A(rs/t) e_p((r-s)*beta(rs/t)/(rs/t)).

In its fourth trace moment use a=rs/t, q=u/r, h=v/s. The color weight is
W=1_A(a)1_A(qa)1_A(qha)1_A(ha). Direct expansion gives phase alpha*r+gamma/r,
where

 alpha=[h beta(a)-h beta(qa)+beta(qha)-beta(ha)]/(h*a),
 gamma=(t/q)[-q beta(a)+beta(qa)-beta(qha)+q beta(ha)].

For q=1 or h=1 both coefficients vanish. These weighted triples number
2d^2-d, where d=|A|.

Now assume q!=1 and h!=1. The two equations for vanishing have coefficient
rows, in the ordered values at a,qa,qha,ha,

                 [ h, -h,  1, -1 ],
                 [-q,  1, -1,  q ].

These rows are independent. If row2 were a multiple of row1, coordinate 3
would force the multiplier to be -1; coordinate 2 would then give h=1 and
coordinate 1 would give q=1, impossible. Their kernel contains

                   (1,1,1,1), (a,qa,qha,ha).

These vectors are independent because a!=qa. Thus the kernel consists
exactly of vectors whose four values are an affine function of the four
slopes. Both coefficients vanish if and only if the four graph points

 (a,beta(a)), (qa,beta(qa)), (qha,beta(qha)), (ha,beta(ha))

are collinear. This remains true when slopes repeat, including q=h=-1;
there is no tacit assumption of four distinct points.

For each ordered pair of distinct palette slopes a,b=qa, their graph points
determine a nonvertical line. A line intersects the graph of beta in at
most D slopes, since beta is genuinely nonlinear of degree D<p. Therefore
c=ha has at most D choices. It then determines h and the fourth slope qha;
the additional palette/collinearity restrictions only reduce the count.
There are at most D*d*(d-1) such exceptional triples.

The total weighted triple count is still at most d^3. The complete sum in r
is a Kloosterman sum, with the same one-coefficient-zero treatment as in the
quadratic audit. Consequently

      tr(M_t^4) <= (D+2)*p*d^2 + 2sqrt(p)*d^3.            (1)

2. COORDINATE AXES

The quotient sigma_a sigma_b^(-1) has slope rho=a/b and intercept

                    beta(rho*b)-rho*beta(b).

If c_D is the leading coefficient of beta, the coefficient of b^D here is
c_D(rho^D-rho). For rho^(D-1)!=1 the polynomial is nonconstant of degree D,
so every prescribed intercept has at most D representing b. There are at
most D-1 other nonzero ratios. Classifying ALL of these ratios as exceptional
is valid even if their quotient polynomial is still nonconstant.

For an exceptional ratio the total number of pairs is at most d, so the
sum of squared quotient multiplicities at that ratio is at most d^2.
For every other ratio, that energy is at most D times the number of pairs.
Summing gives the safe bound

                  E({sigma_a:a in A}) <= (2D-1)d^2.      (2)

The affine permutation character argument from the quadratic proof now
gives an axis norm to the fourth power at most (p-1)(2D-1)d^2.

3. CONSEQUENCE AND HYPOTHESES

Equations (1)-(2) give
lambda=O_D(p^(1/4)d^(1/2)+p^(1/8)d^(3/4)). The exact support identity and
the constant-vector decomposition therefore prove m=O_D(p^(19/10)) for
common domains. The argument in pass4-nested-support-theorem.txt gives
m=O_D(p^(35/18)) for domains ordered by inclusion.

The degree must be genuinely at least two; the collinearity count fails
for an affine-linear beta. The restriction D<p prevents a nonlinear formal
polynomial from representing an affine function on the entire field. A
uniform asymptotic constant is asserted only when D is fixed.

This extension uses the same classical Weil Kloosterman bound as the
quadratic proof: Harcos, Theorem 1, page 1,
https://users.renyi.hu/~gharcos/weil.pdf .

AN OBSTRUCTION TO EDGE-LOCAL TRIANGLE ENCODINGS OF THE FOUR-SLOT SYSTEM
15 October 2026. Hand proof; no finite search or Lean certification.

STATUS AND SCOPE

The exact four-slot removal lemma proposed in
pass2-quadratic-removal-target-audit.txt remains unproved. The result
below excludes a specific natural way of obtaining it: an exact
triangle representation with O(p) vertices in each part in which
each of the four slot values is determined by one triangle edge.

This does NOT disprove the removal lemma, exclude hypergraph or
unbounded-witness representations, or exclude an argument exploiting
the special common, properly oriented support R. It is a counting
obstruction to a general arbitrary-four-support triangle encoding.

1. THE CONFIGURATION SET HAS ORDER p^3

For an odd prime p, let X_p be the set of tuples (a,b,c,d,e,f,g) with
a,d,f nonzero and

    a+b=d+e,    ac=de,    a+c=f+g,    ab=fg.               (1)

Its four ordered-pair slots are

    S_1=(a,b),  S_2=(a,c),  S_3=(d,e),  S_4=(f,g).

Fix pairwise distinct nonzero a,d,f with a^2 != df. The equations
e=a+b-d and g=a+c-f reduce the two product equations to

    d b - a c = d(d-a),
    a b - f c = f(a-f).                                  (2)

The determinant of this linear system in b,c is a^2-df, so it has
exactly one solution. It then determines e,g. For each of the
(p-1)(p-2) choices of a,d there are at least p-4 permissible f:
exclude 0,a,d and the at most one further value a^2/d. Consequently

    |X_p| >= (p-1)(p-2)(p-4).                            (3)

Every configuration just constructed has b!=c. Indeed if b=c,
the sum/product equations give d,f in {a,b}, incompatible with
pairwise distinct a,d,f. Thus (3) also lower-bounds the number of
nondegenerate configurations.

Conversely, fixing a,b,c gives at most two orders for each crossed
root pair (d,e),(f,g), so |X_p| <= 4(p-1)p^2. Hence the complete
configuration system genuinely has the p^3 solution scale.

2. ANY TWO SLOT VALUES HAVE FIBERS OF SIZE AT MOST FOUR

For every distinct i,j in {1,2,3,4}, the map

                 X_p --> (S_i,S_j)

has fibers of size at most four. Explicitly:

  * S_1,S_2 fix a,b,c. Each of S_3,S_4 is an ordered root pair
    with prescribed sum and product: at most 2*2 possibilities.

  * S_1,S_3 fix c=de/a; S_4 has at most two possibilities.

  * S_1,S_4 fix c=f+g-a; S_3 has at most two possibilities.

  * S_2,S_3 fix b=d+e-a; S_4 has at most two possibilities.

  * S_2,S_4 fix b=fg/a; S_3 has at most two possibilities.

  * S_3,S_4 force a to solve

                a^2-(f+g)a+de=0.

    There are at most two a; then b=d+e-a and c=f+g-a are fixed.
    Any remaining equation only reduces this number.

In particular, any subset Y_p of X_p with |Y_p|>=c*p^3 has at
least (c/4)*p^3 distinct joint values for EVERY pair of slots.

3. THE PRECISE TRIANGLE MODEL THAT IS IMPOSSIBLE

Suppose there are three finite vertex sets V_1,V_2,V_3 and a set
of triangles T subset V_1 x V_2 x V_3, together with a surjection

                    pi:T --> Y_p subset X_p.

For each slot i, suppose a fixed triangle edge type E_i in
{12,13,23} and a deterministic edge-label function ell_i satisfy

                  S_i(pi(t)) = ell_i(t restricted to E_i).

This is the usual locality property that permits arbitrary slot
membership restrictions R_i to be enforced by retaining or deleting
edges of a tripartite graph. Multiple triangle lifts of the same
configuration are permitted; pi need not be injective.

Two of the four slots, say i,j, use the same edge type uv. Therefore
their joint image on Y_p has size at most |V_u|*|V_v|. Section 2 gives

                    |Y_p| <= 4 |V_u| |V_v|.              (4)

If |Y_p|=Omega(p^3), (4) contradicts |V_i|=O(p) for all three
parts. In particular this model cannot encode all X_p, or even a
positive proportion of its nondegenerate configurations, at the
ordinary p-vertex triangle-removal scale.

The argument permits arbitrary nonlinear labels and changes of
coordinates. It also survives a bounded number of encoding types:
partition the represented configurations among those types, choose
a type with Omega(p^3) configurations, and apply the same argument.
Allowing a bounded number of possible slot labels per edge merely
changes the constant in (4). Unbounded choices are outside the model.

One necessary cost for this model is an edge universe of size
Omega(p^3). If all three parts have the same order, this requires
Omega(p^(3/2)) vertices in a part. This necessary size increase alone
does not prove that every possible deletion-transfer argument fails;
such an argument would need additional multiplicity control.

4. RELATION TO THE THREE-SLOT COORDINATES

The earlier coordinates U=a,V=a+b,W=ac successfully put S_1 on UV,
S_2 on UW, and the selected root pair S_3 on VW. The fourth root
pair has sum U+W/U and product U(V-U), so it uses all three variables.

Section 3 proves that this defect cannot be repaired merely by
finding another three-coordinate, deterministic edge-local encoding
of all four slots with O(p) choices per coordinate. It does not rule
out a fourth variable, a hypergraph, a growing witness alphabet, or
a structural argument restricted to actual proper B supports.

5. WHAT REMAINS OPEN IN THIS PASS

No uniform four-partite removal theorem at the p^3 scale has been
proved here, and no counterexample to that theorem has been found.
In particular this note supplies no new o(p^2) edge bound for
arbitrary supports of the quadratic affine palette.

ARBITRARY HEAVY MASKS CAN CREATE LINEAR-SCALE NEGATIVE SPECTRUM
15 October 2026, pass 5. Explicit asymptotic construction; no enumeration.

This note rules out a mask-uniform o(p) lower-spectrum estimate for the
support-weighted crossed operator, even for proper quadratic colorings
with Theta(p) colors and Theta(p) entries in every color. The constructed
graphs are NOT asserted to satisfy B. Consequently this does not refute
a genuinely B-dependent support-transfer theorem. The bad test vector
is orthogonal to the actual edge indicator, a further limitation stated
explicitly below.

1. OPERATOR

For sigma_a(x)=ax+a^2 and D_a subset F_p put

 (T_D f)(x,y)=sum_a 1_Da(x)1_Da(sigma_a^-1(y))
                               f(sigma_a^-1(y),sigma_a(x)).

Each summand is self-adjoint: its mask is invariant under the crossed
involution. For any real w,

 <w,T_Dw>=sum_a sum_(x,z in D_a)
                         w(x,sigma_a(z))w(z,sigma_a(x)).       (1)

2. A PROPER DENSE QUADRATIC STARTING GRAPH

Let p tend to infinity through odd primes, set

 k=floor(p/10), s=floor(p/20), A={1,...,k}, X={1,...,2s},
 X_0={1,...,s}, X_1={s+1,...,2s}.

Use these integer representatives as subsets of F_p. The full palette
graph on A x X is properly colored and simple. A collision of distinct
colors a,b at a row x would imply

                ax+a^2=bx+b^2, hence x+a+b=0 in F_p.

But 0<x+a+b<=2s+2k<p. Each individual sigma_a is a permutation, so
properness also holds at columns within each color. Moreover sigma_a(x)
is nonzero, since a and x+a are nonzero modulo p. Hence all columns used
are in Y=F_p*, which has even size p-1.

3. A BALANCED COLUMN PARTITION WITH HEAVY DOMAINS

There is a partition Y=Y_0 disjoint-union Y_1 with equal part sizes and

             |sigma_a(X_i) intersect Y_i|>=s/3
                        for every a in A and i in {0,1}.     (2)

For completeness, label every element of Y independently and fairly by
0 or 1. For a fixed a,i, the count in (2) is Binomial(s,1/2), since
sigma_a is injective on X_i. The elementary multiplicative Chernoff
bound makes the probability that this count is less than s/3 at most
exp(-s/36). A union bound over the 2k tests is at most 2k exp(-s/36).

The probability of exact balance is
binom(p-1,(p-1)/2)/2^(p-1)>=1/p: the central binomial coefficient is the
largest among p coefficients whose sum is 2^(p-1). For sufficiently large
p, 2k exp(-s/36)<1/p. Thus some exactly balanced labeling satisfies every
test. No independence between different tests is needed.

Define

         D_a=(X_0 intersect sigma_a^-1(Y_0))
                    union (X_1 intersect sigma_a^-1(Y_1)).

Retain precisely those color-a incidences. Properness and simplicity
are inherited from the starting graph. Every color has at least 2s/3
entries; the palette and all color classes therefore have linear size.

4. EXACT NEGATIVE QUADRATIC FORM

Take the rank-at-most-two test matrix

             w=1_(X_0 x Y_1)-1_(X_1 x Y_0).

Equal sizes of the two row parts and column parts give

                    sum w=0, ||w||_2^2=s(p-1).              (3)

For x in D_a intersect X_i, the image sigma_a(x) lies in Y_i.
Consequently in (1) the product is -1 exactly when x,z lie in different
row parts, and is zero when they lie in the same part. Thus

 <w,T_Dw>=-2 sum_a |D_a intersect X_0| |D_a intersect X_1|
        <=-2k s^2/9.                                      (4)

Combining (3)--(4), the Rayleigh quotient of a mean-zero test vector is

       <w,T_Dw>/||w||_2^2 <=-2ks/[9(p-1)]=-Omega(p).        (5)

In particular the compression of T_D to mean-zero functions has a
negative eigenvalue of magnitude Omega(p). This wording is deliberate:
T_D has variable degree, so the mean-zero subspace need not be invariant.
Equation (5) alone already defeats any proposed uniform lower bound
<w,T_Dw>>=-o(p)||w||^2 on all mean-zero w.

5. WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT RULE OUT

The masks create a two-block obstruction. All actual edges lie in
(X_0 x Y_0) union (X_1 x Y_1), whereas w is supported on the off-diagonal
blocks. Thus <1_E,w>=0. A worst-eigenvalue bound throws away this relevant
relationship between the edge set and the masks. The example does not
rule out a lower bound specifically for <1_E,T_D1_E> using that relation.

The example also is not B-colored. In fact, the new common row-column
rectangle theorem in the companion note shows that the two dense actual
diagonal blocks cannot both evade repeated-color squares. This is fully
consistent with (5): a large negative mode can exist in a direction
irrelevant to the actual edge indicator.

Accordingly the obstruction is limited but exact: completed-map expansion
and proper heavy masks alone do not imply the desired lower-spectrum
estimate. A successful general argument must retain B or the actual-edge
alignment, rather than treating all support masks as spectral noise.

FIXED-ROOT-PAIR FIBERS: AN EXACT GENUS-ONE CHECKPOINT
2026-10-15. Analytic calculation; no novelty claim.

This records a paused algebraic direction. The positive common-domain result
in pass4-crossed-operator-independent-audit.txt takes priority.

Work over a field of characteristic different from 2. The four-slot variety
has root pairs (a,b),(a,c),(d,e),(f,g) and equations

 a+b=d+e, ac=de, a+c=f+g, ab=fg.

Fix a!=0 and b, and write r=b/a, u=d/a, v=f/a. Eliminating c,e,g on the open
set f!=0 gives the following plane equation for the remaining fiber:

       v^2 + [u^2-(1+r)u-1]v + r = 0.                   (1)

Indeed e/a=1+r-u, g/a=r/v, and c/a=u(1+r-u); the equality
1+c/a=v+r/v is exactly (1). Put B_r(u)=u^2-(1+r)u-1 and y=2v+B_r(u). Then

                     y^2=B_r(u)^2-4r.                  (2)

When r!=0, a repeated root of the quartic on the right must satisfy
B_r'(u)=0 and B_r(u)^2=4r. Thus u=(1+r)/2, and the repeated-root condition is

 (r^2+2r+5)^2-64r = (r-1)^2(r^2+6r+25) = 0.

Consequently, if r!=0, r!=1, and r^2+6r+25!=0, the quartic is squarefree.
Its smooth projective double cover of P^1 is branched at four simple points,
and has genus one by Riemann-Hurwitz. The point (u,v)=(1,1), equivalently
(u,y)=(1,1-r), is rational and smooth under these hypotheses. Thus the smooth
projective fiber is an elliptic curve after choosing that point as origin.

There is no nonconstant rational one-parameter parametrization of this
generic fixed-(a,b) fiber, even over an algebraic closure. A map from P^1 to
its smooth genus-one model would contradict Riemann-Hurwitz after factoring
off its inseparable Frobenius part when necessary. This excludes rational
families that keep this root pair fixed; it does not exclude families that
vary a and b, or finite-field recurrence arguments using arbitrary supports.

The two obvious root swaps are u -> 1+r-u and v -> r/v. They commute and
generate at most a Klein four group on this fiber, so these swaps by
themselves do not supply an infinite recurrence orbit.

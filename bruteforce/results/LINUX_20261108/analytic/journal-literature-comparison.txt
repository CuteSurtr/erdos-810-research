MAIN CONSEQUENCE FOR THIS PROJECT

The p^(-3/4) random-potential threshold has a published general framework:
Chen-Greenhill (2021), and also Kim-Lund-Pham-Yoo (2023). Our construction's
specific counts and overlap estimates supply the hypotheses. The possible
contribution is therefore an algebraic application, not a new general
threshold method. The earlier estimate of 15-20 substantive result families
in the archive was NOT a count of historically novel theorems.

The strongest remaining candidates are the explicit quadratic operator
factorization and its actual-column-support consequence, and the older
fixed-formula quadratic application. Their underlying character-sum and
regularity machinery is established. This search did not identify an exact
published statement subsuming those applications, but that does not prove
novelty, correctness of every archived lemma, or publication significance.

The search also prompted a new deduction within the project: the previously
open distinct-potential-set count is now proved in potential-set-orbits.txt.
Its generic labeling argument supplies an explicit arithmetic critical-window
law when combined with Chen-Greenhill. This is an application candidate,
not an assertion that the general probabilistic machinery is new.

EXACT TRANSLATION FOR THE NEW THRESHOLD COMPARISON

Use the random family and notation of random-potential-threshold.txt.
Let F be the collection of distinct four-potential sets of three-color
cycles, and M_p its cardinality. The written proofs give

   N_4pot~p^3/64,  N_4pot/72<=M_p<=N_4pot,
   Delta_1<=p^2, Delta_2<=108p, Delta_3<=216, Delta_4<=72.

Therefore M_p=Theta(p^3). The same upper bounds apply after deduplication.
In Chen-Greenhill's notation take q=p, n=1, a=4, b=3, c=0. Sampling the
whole field and intersecting with J realizes our original H distribution.
This verifies their hypotheses, and their threshold evaluates to p^(-3/4).
The O(p^2) three-potential and two-color exceptional cycles vanish below
this scale by the first moment. This is a concrete application of their
theorem, not a claim that the paper studies this particular coloring.

The additional Poisson conclusion gives X_p -> Poisson(lambda) whenever
M_p eta^4 -> lambda in (0,infinity), with X_p counting DISTINCT sets.
The exceptional obstructions also vanish at that scale, so the rainbow
probability tends to exp(-lambda). Appendix B supplies the full short
deduction. The companion potential-set-orbits.txt now establishes
M_p=N_4pot/g+O(p^2), g=gcd(3,p-1), by controlling nontrivial permutations
of the potential slots. This supplies the leading constant 1/(64g).
For eta=c p^(-3/4), the explicit rainbow probability tends to
exp(-c^4/(64g)), along either fixed prime residue class. Raw cycles converge
to g times a Poisson(c^4/(64g)) variable, because exceptional orbit
collisions and three-potential cycles vanish with high probability.
The general limit theorem is attributed to Chen-Greenhill; the slot
classification and its consequence are new to this project, with broader
historical priority unverified.
